# Hydrology comparison and baseline

`river-donja-42.png`: actual browser-rendered 512² terrain raster from the original #42 code (left) and river geometry change (right), same Donja Bukovica seed 42 and entire 40 × 40 km footprint. Temporary test copies exposed the existing terrain canvas at fixed 512 CSS pixels; they did not alter terrain or rendering algorithms. The screenshot crops are presented side by side at the same scale. Crew markers, coordinate labels and weather are omitted because the comparison shows the terrain raster, not the whole game interface. The screenshot is one example, not the required multi-region visual validation. The blue areas are potential filled basins, not calibrated permanent lakes.

`../terrain-validation/cartography-baseline.json`: 13 region/seed combinations × 2 generations from base `9fc3ddf`; 13/13 hashes match `water-mask-fixed-seeds.json`. Node v26.12.1, Intel i7-12700H, 20 logical CPUs; other work including browsers ran concurrently. Full worker-handler VM times: first runs 6.0–17.8 s, median 9.53 s; this is not a browser benchmark and is affected by contention. Generation completed, deterministic fields, flow to edge on filled surface and conservation checks passed. Runtime and source hash are in JSON. Prior browser timing from `TERRAIN-REPORT.md` is a separate measurement.

Lake investigation: priority flood raises depressions to a spill elevation regardless of evaporation, infiltration, groundwater or duration; mask seeds all cells deeper than 0.4 m and then connects shallow shelves. For Mckhetovac/4294967295 the display mask covers 17.6% of the map. This is not evidence of permanent water. Wetland selection uses slope and filled-depth/accumulation thresholds, not an independently calibrated water balance. A defensible permanent-lake classifier needs additional assumptions and validation; neither a cosmetic cutoff nor treating all filled basins as lakes resolves this.

`river-mckhetovac-max.png`: the same browser comparison for Mckhetovac/4294967295. It demonstrates that the new line geometry does not address the very large potential basins. These remain a critical unresolved visual and model issue.

USGS distinguishes a depression-filled drainage surface from actual persistent lakes; lake storage depends on atmospheric, surface and groundwater inputs and outputs. Sources: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/efficient-delineation-nested-depression-hierarchy-digital-elevation-models and https://www.usgs.gov/publications/hydrological-processes-and-water-budget-lakes. This generator has no independently calibrated lake evaporation, infiltration, groundwater or storage time series, so a depth cutoff alone cannot prove that a basin is a lake or a wetland. Any replacement must state assumptions and ensure dry channel routing remains physically coherent.

The physical-elevation audit (`node tests/river-matrix.mjs docs/terrain-validation/river-geometry-matrix.json`) counts each river edge whose *ground* receiver is over 1 mm higher. It separately counts edges touching displayed water and those entirely outside it; the priority-flood filled surface still passes the non-increase invariant. In Donja/42, 118 uphill edges are outside the lake mask and 1052 touch water. The dry case contradicts the desired no-uphill condition; a visual curve is not a fix. Until the hydrology/land-water classification is corrected, the new river rendering is experimental and #43 must remain draft.

The geometry matrix completed for four regions × seeds 0, 42 and 4294967295. All active graph edges had corresponding display halves, all tested control points remained within half a cell of the graph node, and no crossing diagonal receiver pairs were found in those 12 grids. Dry uphill edges ranged from 25 to 233 per map, so this is an audit exposing failures, not a passing no-uphill test. The analysis does not prove that smoothed curves cannot cross across all possible terrain.

Inspection of Mckhetovac/4294967295 also found several connected potential basins thousands of grid cells large, with mean filled depths around 85–129 m. Priority flood fills these to spill elevation without time or volume restrictions. Thus both the enormous blue areas and apparent flow through them reflect the conditioning assumption, not validated lakes.

Reproduce the basin inspection with `node tests/basin-audit.mjs mckhetovac 4294967295`. Results are saved in `../terrain-validation/basin-audit-mckhetovac-max.json`. This groups adjacent displayed mask cells whose inferred filled-surface elevations differ by at most 0.01 m; it is an exploratory grouping, not a nested-depression hierarchy. `area` is grid cells, `maxq` is the maximum normalized accumulation within that group (not measured discharge), `ratio` is `maxq / area`, and `meanDepth` is the mean analysis fill depth in metres. Shoreline seam generalization can split these groups, so they must not be interpreted as exact lake inventories or independent water budgets.

Experimental `surface-flow.js` computes strictly downhill D8 ground flow, leaves interior minima as sinks, and conserves runoff. It is **not connected to the game** because it has no reservoir filling, flat routing or spill; replacing priority-flood drainage directly would create false river terminations. Synthetic tests: `node --test --test-isolation=none tests/surface-flow.test.mjs`. `node tests/surface-flow-audit.mjs donja_bukovica 42` on a 512² worker-generated map found 425 interior sinks, 112 with >600 uniformly weighted contributing cells, zero uphill edges and no crossing diagonal pairs for this one sample. Its unit runoff differs from the climate-weighted game network; counts are not directly comparable. One VM pass took ~160 ms on this host under concurrent load, not an isolated benchmark.

For the existing network on Donja/42, `node tests/river-sink-audit.mjs` finds 44 of 118 dry uphill source cells have no strictly lower neighbour (1 mm tolerance). Of the 118, 112 have no strictly lower, earlier flood-ranked neighbour on the same filled level, and three more have only a crossing diagonal candidate. An experiment favouring descending ground in priority-flood flats inside the erosion loop increased the dry-uphill count and produced diagonal crossings; rejecting crossings still worsened several cases. Neither experiment was integrated. A final-routing-only variant reduced Donja/0 from 120 to 115, left Donja/42 at 118 and changed network accumulation, so it too was rejected. These are sample observations, not proofs for all seeds.
